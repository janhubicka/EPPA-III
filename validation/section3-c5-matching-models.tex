\textbf{Policy.} The author manuscript remains frozen.
This note refines the correction register C25.

Suppose an order-ten transitive EPPA host \(H\) of \(C_5\)
has one outside vertex with exactly one selected neighbour.
The verified cyclic saturation theorem gives a unique outside
vertex \(b_x\) with selected-neighbourhood profile \(\{x\}\).
No identification of the two halves is assumed.

In these coordinates the outside graph is \(C_5\) or
\(\overline{C_5}\) by the genuine action of EPPA rotation lifts
and equality of the two internal edge counts. Hence the whole
host has one of the following two explicit adjacency patterns:
\[
\begin{split}
 (0,i)\sim(0,j)&\iff j-i\equiv\pm1\pmod5,\\
 (1,i)\sim(1,j)&\iff
 \begin{cases}j-i\equiv\pm1\pmod5&\text{prism},\\
 j-i\equiv\pm2\pmod5&\text{Petersen},\end{cases}\\
 (0,i)\sim(1,j)&\iff i=j .
\end{split}
\]

The module \texttt{CycleFiveMatchingModels.lean} constructs the
actual equivalence identifying the host with a canonical model.
Its main declaration is
\texttt{cycleFive\_eppa\_singleton\_host\_two\_matching\_graphs}.
The new declaration counts as certified only upon successful
GitHub Lean CI and permitted-axiom audit.
