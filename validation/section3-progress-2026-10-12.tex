\paragraph{Localized Claim 3.9 correction.} For $r\ge1$ coordinates of
the proper size $0<k<t$, the wreath orbit of the neighbourhood profile
has at least $\binom sr\binom tk^r\ge s\binom tk$ elements.
EPPA realizes all these among exactly $st$ outside vertices, so
$\binom tk\le t$ and $k=1$ or $k=t-1$. At saturation outside profiles
are distinct, hence extension actions there are uniquely determined.
