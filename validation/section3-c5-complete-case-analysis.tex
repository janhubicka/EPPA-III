\paragraph{Scope and proof boundary.}
This is an independent finite audit and proposed
source-local proof simplification. It is not a complete
Lean validation of Lemma~3.18; the original manuscript
remains frozen.

Write \(P_w=N_H(w)\cap V(C_5)\) for outside vertices.
The verified cyclic profile-saturation theorem shows that
either all profiles are empty/full, or the five profiles
are exactly the five translates of one nonempty proper
subset and the cross-degree \(k=|P_w|\) is uniform.
The empty/full case gives \(2C_5\) or its complement
by degree-rigidity and connected-component transport.
Complementing the entire EPPA pair, and relabelling
\(\overline{C_5}\cong C_5\) by multiplication by two,
sends \(k\) to \(5-k\). Thus the proper cases reduce,
up to permitted complementation, to \(k=1,2\).

For \(k=1\), the cross edges form a perfect matching.
The complementary half is \(C_5\) or \(\overline{C_5}\).
The two hosts are the prism and Petersen graphs,
respectively; the Petersen graph is primitive and
is excluded by the manuscript's imprimitive hypothesis.
For \(k=2\), profiles are the five translated adjacent
pairs or the five translated diagonal pairs. The verified
degree-two host theorem reduces the actual host to
the two surviving canonical cyclic doubles; the
complementary-top alternatives violate triangle-count
equality. Their isomorphisms with the named Taylor-double
and complemented clique-extension models are still Lean
obligations.

\paragraph{Short Petersen primitivity argument.}
In the model as the two-subsets of a five-element set,
with edges between disjoint subsets, its automorphism
group is transitive on its \(15\) edges and on its
\(30\) nonedges. An invariant partition into
size-two or size-five blocks would contribute
respectively \(5\) or \(20\) unordered vertex pairs
inside blocks. Neither is a union of orbits of
sizes \(15,30\), so neither partition exists.
The script independently verifies primitivity by
enumerating the action on all candidate blocks.
