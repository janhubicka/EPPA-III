\section*{Section 3, \(C_5\): excluding Petersen in the actual imprimitive host}
\textbf{Manuscript policy:} this note is stored only in the
Lean verification repository.

The matching branch of Lemma~3.18 starts with an actual
order-ten transitive EPPA host \(H\) of the designated
\(C_5\), with at least one singleton outside profile.
The established graph-level theorem gives an actual
equivalence \(\varphi:\{0,1\}\times\mathrm{Fin}\,5
\longrightarrow V(H)\), fixing the selected copy, under
which \(H\) is one of the two canonical matching models.
The prism is admissible; the other model is Petersen.

Suppose \(H\) has a nontrivial invariant partition
\(\pi:V(H)\twoheadrightarrow B\). Define
\(\pi'(x)=\pi(\varphi(x))\) on the canonical vertex type.
Every automorphism of the transported graph is conjugate
under \(\varphi\) to an automorphism of \(H\), hence
\(\pi'\) is invariant. Vertex transitivity and surjectivity
also transport. The numbers of blocks and their fibre
sizes are preserved exactly.

For the Petersen alternative, the previously certified
root-fixing automorphism has vertex orbits of orders
\(1,3,6\). Consequently every block containing the root
has size \(1,4,7\), or \(10\). A nontrivial invariant
partition of a transitive ten-vertex graph has all
blocks of size \(2\) or \(5\); contradiction.
Thus the actual host must be the prism.

The proposed reusable Lean lemma
\texttt{invariantBlockMap\_comap\_equiv} transports
arbitrary invariant block maps along graph equivalences.
The main application
\texttt{cycleFive\_eppa\_singleton\_imprimitive\_host\_is\_prism}
proves the prism conclusion under the exact formal
partition hypotheses (surjective invariant block map,
at least two blocks and a block containing distinct
vertices). It assumes no predetermined outside indexing
and no coherent EPPA extensions.
