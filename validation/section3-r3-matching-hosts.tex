\section*{EPPA--III, Lemma 3.20: actual matching rook hosts}
\textbf{Policy.} Source-local validation note only;
the circulated manuscript stays frozen.

For an eighteen-vertex vertex-transitive EPPA witness
\(H\) of the nine-vertex rook graph \(R_3\), a single
outside singleton profile forces a unique matching
between the designated rook vertices and all outside
vertices. This is a \emph{conclusion} of EPPA, not an
assumed identification of the two halves.

The outside induced graph is \(R_3\) or its complement
under those singleton labels. Thus the entire host is
one of the two graphs on \(\{0,1\}\times V(R_3)\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
 (0,x)\sim(0,y)&\iff x\sim_{R_3}y,\\
 (1,x)\sim(1,y)&\iff
    \begin{cases}x\sim_{R_3}y&\text{model 0},\\
    x\ne y,\; x\nsim_{R_3}y&\text{model 1},\end{cases}\\
 (0,x)\sim(1,y)&\iff x=y.
\end{aligned}
\]
The proposed Lean module
\texttt{RookThreeMatchingModels.lean} constructs an
actual equivalence \(\varphi:\{0,1\}\times V(R_3)
\simeq V(H)\) fixing the original embedded copy
and proves equality of the transported adjacency
with exactly one of these explicit matching graphs.

These models correspond to the two actions on paired
halves considered in the manuscript, but the precise
isomorphism with each named alternative must be
checked rather than assumed from an informal description.
The degree-zero/full and complementary cross-degree
branches of Lemma~3.20 also remain separate.

\paragraph{Localized proposed wording.}
In the cross-degree-one case, index the outside
vertices by their unique selected neighbour.
Genuine EPPA lifts force the rook automorphism action
on these labels. Their matching cross relation and the
two invariant top graphs then determine the entire host,
without presupposing equivariant outside labels.
