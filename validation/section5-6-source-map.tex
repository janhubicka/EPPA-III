\paragraph{Double-cover core.}
The theorem
\texttt{transitive\_eppa\_double\_cover\_classification}
combines the actual-host two-point block reduction, a canonical
equivalence $\{0,1\}\times V(G)\simeq V(H)$ fixing the embedded copy,
Taylor adjacency between distinct fibres, optional uniform mate edges,
and transitivity on ordered inter-block edges and nonedges.
It assumes a non-homogeneous base, the non-cluster exclusions,
and an actual transitive imprimitive EPPA host; no coherent lifts or
pre-indexed outside layer.

\paragraph{Verification boundary.}
The source statement excludes Gardiner's homogeneous graph families,
rather than explicitly assuming non-homogeneity of $G$.
That bridge is not part of this Lean theorem. The shorter verified
two-point proof does not certify every line of the original
maximal-partial-map argument in Claim~5.14.
