\paragraph{Scope.}
Let \(H\) be a ten-vertex vertex-transitive graph with an
induced EPPA embedding \(e:C_5\hookrightarrow H\).
Assume \emph{one} vertex outside \(e(C_5)\) has exactly
one selected neighbour. No outside block partition,
equivariant labels or uniform cross-degree are assumed.
The source is frozen; these are proposed local
proof replacements only.

\section*{The matching is a conclusion of EPPA}
The five nonempty singleton subsets of \(V(C_5)\)
form one rotation orbit of size five. By EPPA all five
occur as profiles of outside vertices. Since the
complement contains exactly five vertices, they each
occur once. Equivalently, there is a unique outside
vertex \(b_x\) with selected-neighbourhood profile
\(P_{b_x}=\{x\}\) for every \(x\in V(C_5)\).
These vertices give a genuine outside equivalence
\(f:\mathrm{Fin}\,5\simeq B\), and the entire cross graph
is the perfect matching
\[
     H(e(x),f(y))\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x=y.
\]
Every rotation \(\rho\in\operatorname{Aut}(C_5)\) has
an EPPA lift preserving the selected and outside halves.
The lift necessarily maps \(b_x\) to \(b_{\rho(x)}\),
because their selected-neighbourhood profiles are unique.
Thus the outside graph in these coordinates is invariant
under the cyclic rotations. The four possibilities are
empty, \(C_5\), complementary \(C_5\) or complete.
Vertex-transitivity and the equality of the internal
edge counts of the two equal halves leave exactly the
two five-cycle possibilities.

\paragraph{Next structural distinction.}
If the top graph is \(C_5\) under these labels,
the host is the prism \(C_5\square K_2\).
If the top graph is complementary \(C_5\),
the host is the Petersen graph; this host is primitive
and hence excluded from Lemma~3.18, which assumes
imprimitivity. This last exclusion can be certified
independently by checking the invariant blocks under
the ten-vertex Petersen automorphism group, as in
\texttt{checks/section3\_c5\_host\_patterns.py}.

\paragraph{Proposed local wording.}
\begin{quote}
If the cross-degree is one, EPPA carries any
singleton neighbourhood to all five singleton
profiles, each represented by a unique outside
vertex. Indexing the outside by its singleton
neighbour gives a perfect matching between the
halves. This indexing is preserved by the lifts
of base rotations. The induced outside graph
has five edges and is rotation-invariant, hence
is \(C_5\) or its complement. The corresponding
hosts are the prism and Petersen graph; the
latter is primitive and does not occur here.
\end{quote}
