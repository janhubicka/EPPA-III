\section*{EPPA--III Section 3 remainder: audit}
The manuscript source \texttt{main-new(7).tex} remains unchanged.
\paragraph{S3-01, Lemma 3.12: false without non-homogeneous witness.}
Take \(G=K_2\dot\cup K_1\subset H=3K_2\). Then \(H\) is homogeneous,
transitive and imprimitive, and \(2|G|=|H|\), but is not one of
the graphs in the conclusion. More strongly
\(K_3\dot\cup K_2\dot\cup K_1\subset 4K_3\) contradicts \(s=2\).
Add the hypothesis that \(H\) is non-homogeneous; this is necessary,
not yet a complete repair of the proof.
\paragraph{S3-02, Proposition 3.23: the doubles are missing.}
Both \(2C_5\) and \(2R_3\) are non-homogeneous, transitive and imprimitive,
and each is an EPPA witness for one embedded \(C_5\) or \(R_3\).
They are incorrectly called homogeneous in the proof.
Case (4) should include \(H=2G\), as does the main theorem.
\paragraph{S3-03, Proposition 3.23: invalid invocation of Lemma 3.3.}
The final paragraph invokes Lemma 3.3 for complements of
unions of cliques, which that lemma explicitly excludes.
Complement both \(G\) and \(H\) and use the equal-clique
case, after settling the partition hypothesis below.
\paragraph{S3-04, Proposition 3.23: missing partition hypothesis.}
Lemma 3.7 assumes not only that \(G\cong sK_t\) but also that
\(H\setminus G\cong sK_t\). Proposition 3.23 invokes Lemma 3.7
without first showing the latter property. Regularity and
\(|G|=|H|/2\) give equal edge counts in the two halves,
not isomorphism. The \(s=t=2\) instance is checked separately;
the general transfer remains open.
\paragraph{S3-05, Claim 3.15: structural inference needs repair.}
An edge between different blocks of imprimitivity need not
automatically join two blocks meeting \(G\). Also extending
permutations of \(G\cap\Pi\) while fixing a vertex of \(\Pi'\)
does not by itself establish a faithful action on
\(\Pi'\setminus G\). Hence the claimed inequality
\(|\Pi'\setminus G|\geq |G\cap\Pi|\) requires an argument,
as do the subsequent equality and two-block conclusions.
This is a proof gap, not a known counterexample when \(H\)
is assumed non-homogeneous.
