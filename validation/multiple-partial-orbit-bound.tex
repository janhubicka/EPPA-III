\paragraph{Purpose.}
This is a source-local supporting argument for replacing the
problematic regularity and simultaneous-matching discussion in
Claims~3.8--3.11 of the supplied
\texttt{main-new(7).tex}. It is \emph{not} a change to the
manuscript and is \emph{not yet} a complete Lean proof of
Lemma~3.7.

\paragraph{Lemma.}
Let \(G=sK_t\), with \(s,t\ge2\), be induced in an
EPPA witness \(H\) on \(2st\) vertices.
For \(w\in H\setminus G\), let
\[
 P_i=N_H(w)\cap C_i,\qquad
 J=\{i:0<|P_i|<t\},\qquad p=|J|.
\]
If \(p\ge2\), then \(s=t=p=2\).

\paragraph{Proof.}
Write \(W=S_t\wr S_s=\operatorname{Aut}(G)\).
For every \(p\)-subset \(R\subseteq[s]\), choose a
permutation of the \(s\) clique indices taking \(J\)
to \(R\). Under this permutation the \(p\) selected
coordinates have some sizes \(k_i\in[1,t-1]\).
For each \(i\in R\), independent permutations of
the \(t\) vertices of \(C_i\) can take \(P_i\)
to any \(k_i\)-subset. There are
\(\binom{t}{k_i}\ge t\) such subsets.
Fixing the index permutation for each \(R\)
and varying these selected subsets gives at least
\(t^p\) distinct profiles with \(J=R\).
Different \(R\)'s yield disjoint profile families,
because the set of partial-coordinate indices is
recovered from the profile. Hence
\[
       |W\cdot P(w)|\ge\binom{s}{p}t^p. \tag{1}
\]
EPPA makes every translated profile realised by
a vertex of \(H\setminus G\), giving
\[
       \binom{s}{p}t^p\le st. \tag{2}
\]
If \(2\le p<s\), then
\(\binom{s}{p}\ge s\), while
\(t^p>t\), contradicting (2).
If \(p=s\), (2) becomes \(t^{s-1}\le s\).
For \(s\ge3\) and \(t\ge2\),
\[
 t^{s-1}\ge 2^{s-1}\ge 2(s-1)>s,
\]
again a contradiction. For \(s=2\),
\(t^{s-1}\le s\) gives \(t\le2\).
Thus \(s=t=p=2\). \(\square\)

\paragraph{Relationship to the Wagner exception.}
When \(s=t=p=2\), the two partial coordinates
each consist of a singleton. The corresponding
profile orbit has four elements, naturally indexed
by the four corners of a square.
The induced graph on the other four vertices
is then constrained by the equal-edge-count
identity, recovering the exceptional Wagner
pattern. The present lemma supplies the
\emph{obstruction step}; the final four-vertex
orbit classification is a separate argument.
