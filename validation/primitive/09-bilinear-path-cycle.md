# Bilinear forms constituent reduction

The replacement applies to frozen R17. It supersedes only the bilinear
portion of the combined Grassmann and bilinear proposal. In the elementary
matrix model of the bilinear forms graph, the first constituent has an
induced four-vertex path and the second has an induced five-cycle. The
displayed row pairs work over every field when there are at least three
coordinates. Rank two is witnessed by minors equal to 1 or −1.

The path rules out the clique-union orientations of the first constituent.
The cycle rules out all four reduced shapes of the second constituent.
Their orders exceed nine, so the Gardiner list also rules out homogeneity.
The first constituent must therefore be a cover up to complement. Its even
order forces odd q, in which case the other constituent has even order too.
The exact-half reduction then requires the second constituent to have a
cover shape, contradicting the five-cycle. The proof uses the manuscript's
constituent-shape reduction and the external identification of the
bilinear graph with the matrix model.
