# The rook classification and the order-120 constituents

An induced pair of disjoint rook edges has either equal or different coordinate directions. A partial graph automorphism extends if it respects the two coordinate equalities globally, possibly after transposing. Each required equality comparison is detected by two edges. Pairs sharing a vertex have their relative directions determined by whether the third edge is present. For disjoint edges, a failure to determine relative directions occurs only for induced matchings. Consequently the sole obstruction is having induced matchings of both directions in the same selection.

In the four by four rook graph, a selection of at least eight cells which avoids one of the matching directions lies inside a coordinate image of the three by three corner and opposite singleton or the two-column stripe. The proof counts cells on rows and columns. The reverse implication uses the two explicit positive constructions. Lean proves the criterion for the actual selected embedding in both directions. The stand-alone enumeration in `checks/section8_rook_four.py` remains a second verification. The same positive corner in the five by five rook graph attains the separately proved upper bound of eleven.

In the order-120 model, label the binary coordinates by eight-bit masks, with the first coordinate of each half set in the root 17. The neighbouring vertices with masks 35, 54, 43, 197 and 139 induce a five-cycle in the listed order. The distinct nonneighbours 19, 34, 23, 68 and 98 induce another. Lean checks the constituent memberships and all ten pairs in each cycle. Transitivity moves these cycles to any chosen root.

A five-cycle cannot embed in a clique union because adjacency through a common vertex forces a chord. In a generalized clique cover, there is a cycle edge on one side. The two vertices immediately outside that edge distinguish its endpoints. They must lie on the opposite side of the same block since off-diagonal adjacency is constant. This again forces a chord. Self-complementarity of the five-cycle also gives both complementary exclusions. It follows that neither constituent of the order-120 model has any of the four remaining shapes in the manuscript reduction. This argument uses host transitivity and the previously separated constituent-shape reduction.
